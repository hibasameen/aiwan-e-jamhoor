A note on 2024. Weeks before the vote, PTI lost its shared electoral symbol — the cricket bat — when the Supreme Court upheld the ECP ruling that its intra-party elections were invalid. Its candidates contested as independents, each under a different ballot symbol, and that is how the returns record them: the 99 independent seats above are overwhelmingly PTI-backed. Most of those winners later joined the Sunni Ittehad Council in pursuit of reserved seats. 2024 party totals are therefore not comparable with earlier elections.
