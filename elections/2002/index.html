Pakistan election results 2002

National Assembly · Election date: 10 October 2002 · 270 constituency records

Parliament had been dissolved after the coup of 12 October 1999; the Supreme Court validated the takeover but required elections within three years. Pervez Musharraf took a five-year presidential term through a referendum in April 2002, then issued the Legal Framework Order in August, amending the constitution before the poll. Candidates were required to hold a bachelor's degree, with madrasa certificates treated as equivalent, and anyone who had twice been prime minister was barred; Benazir Bhutto and Nawaz Sharif were both abroad and did not contest. Separate electorates for religious minorities were abolished and the voting age was lowered from 21 to 18. Polling in NA-262 and NA-263 was re-run in November.

How to read this archive

These are the results recorded in this archive, which combines election returns with later corrections, recounts and tribunal outcomes. They are not a certified election-day return or a statement of who currently holds the seat.

Seat numbers and boundaries change between elections. This table covers the constituency records available in the archive; it is not the total membership of the National Assembly. Missing values are shown as unavailable.
