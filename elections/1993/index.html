Pakistan election results 1993

National Assembly · Election date: 6 October 1993 · 202 constituency records

President Ghulam Ishaq Khan dismissed Nawaz Sharif's government in April 1993 under Article 58(2)(b); the Supreme Court reinstated it, and in July the army brokered the resignation of both men. Moeen Qureshi, a former World Bank official with no domestic base, led the caretaker government, which published lists of loan defaulters and tax delinquents and required candidates to declare their own. Around 150,000 troops were deployed and judicial officers replaced civil servants as presiding officers. The MQM boycotted the National Assembly poll while contesting the provincial assemblies, and turnout in Karachi fell to roughly 15 per cent. No party won a majority; Benazir Bhutto formed a coalition on 19 October. A Commonwealth group and an NDI delegation both observed, but neither report is available online.

How to read this archive

These are the results recorded in this archive, which combines election returns with later corrections, recounts and tribunal outcomes. They are not a certified election-day return or a statement of who currently holds the seat.

Seat numbers and boundaries change between elections. This table covers the constituency records available in the archive; it is not the total membership of the National Assembly. Missing values are shown as unavailable.
