Result vintage and source differences

These are the results recorded in this archive, which combines election returns with later corrections, recounts and tribunal outcomes. They are not a certified election-day return or a statement of who currently holds the seat.

The 2024 candidate snapshot was compiled from ElectionPakistani in July 2026 and supplemented with ECP Form 47 figures where available. It includes the postponed NA-8 poll and later result changes. Candidate votes and the available official turnout figures can therefore refer to different stages of the result.

Form 47 transcription notes: The source field “form date” is illegible. Figures above retain the explorer’s recorded values; flagged totals have not been recalculated.

Sources and definitions

Candidate names, party labels and votes are retained from the published 2024 candidate dataset. Vote shares use the total of those candidate votes and are rounded to one decimal place. The margin is expressed in percentage points. Party labels in this table follow the CSV source; the explorer may use expanded labels.
